Business Challenge

Create a clear management view of sales performance across time, product categories and geographic markets, while reducing the effort required to interpret raw transactional data.

What I Built

  • Executive KPI dashboard for revenue, profit, orders, AOV and margin.
  • Monthly revenue trend analysis to identify performance changes over time.
  • Category and state performance views for commercial comparison.
  • Supporting summary tables for validation and reporting.
  • Power BI-ready data structure and DAX measure definitions.

Tools & Skills Demonstrated

Excel / Numbers Power BI SQL Data Cleaning KPI Design Business Analysis Dashboard Development

Business Takeaways

  • Overall profitability is 21.1% across 6,000 orders.
  • Category and state analysis provides a basis for identifying high-value markets.
  • Monthly trend analysis can support sales planning and performance monitoring.

Project Outcome

The project converts raw sales transactions into an executive-friendly reporting solution that can be presented to business stakeholders and extended into an interactive Power BI dashboard.
